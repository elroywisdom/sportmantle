## Page 3: Three Years of Impact
*Sportmantle Magazine | Page 2*

### Impact Timeline (Last 3 Years)
Over the last 3 years, we have held meetings, outreaches, and football tournaments for young athletes in and around Abuja:

- **When Athletes Pray 1.0 (Zuba)**: Our first meeting held in Zuba with attendance of athletes across FCT and Nasarawa.
- **July 2024 — When Athletes Pray 2.0**: Second edition held at Bible Guest House, Zone 5, Wuse, Abuja.
- **October 2024 — AFLAME**: A Holy Ghost meeting in the city of Abuja with athletes gathering from all over.
- **March — Horvel-Prime Outreach**: Outreach to Horvel-Prime Football Academy in Nyanya, FCT, Abuja, where the gospel was preached, questions were answered, and football items were donated.
- **March 2025 — 2-Year Anniversary**: To celebrate God's faithfulness and mercy to us as a ministry, we held a teaching meeting titled *"SPIRITUALS: The Righteousness of God"*.
- **May 2025 — 6-Hour Prayer Stretch**: Held at Adullam, Gudu, Abuja. The Holy Spirit moved in hearts and gave instructions in righteousness.
- **June 2025 — 7-a-Side Young Tournament**: First SportMantle 7-a-side youth tournament hosted in Nasarawa State in partnership with Karu Football Academy.

## Page 4: Junior Tournament 2025 — New Karu
*Sportmantle Magazine | Page 3*

### Tournament, 2025
**Junior Tournament, New Karu | June 2025**

![Junior Tournament Team](magazine_assets/page_04_img_01.jpeg)

In June 2025, SportMantle successfully hosted its highly anticipated Junior Tournament in New Karu, bringing together young talents for a vibrant display of skill, teamwork, and sportsmanship. The tournament featured four dynamic football teams, each demonstrating remarkable determination, discipline, and passion for the game.

The event was more than just a competition; it was a celebration of youth development and community engagement. Throughout the tournament, players showcased not only their athletic abilities but also the core values SportMantle stands for: **character, unity, resilience, and faith-driven excellence**. Coaches and mentors provided guidance both on and off the field, reinforcing lessons of teamwork, respect, and leadership.

The Junior Tournament in New Karu stands as another milestone in our commitment to nurturing the next generation of athletes—physically, mentally, and spiritually. As reflected in the accompanying images, the four participating teams embodied the heart of SportMantle: developing champions on the field and leaders in life.

We look forward to building on this success and expanding opportunities for young athletes in future editions of the tournament.

## Page 9: Faith, Character, Purpose
*Sportmantle Magazine | Page 8*

### **FAITH, CHARACTER, PURPOSE.**

![Victor Gidado - Faith](magazine_assets/page_09_img_01.jpeg)

Victor emphasized that beyond football training, the spiritual mentorship he received shaped his perspective during both victories and challenges:

> *"During the glory days and when faced with challenges, my eyes and heart were enlightened to know the purpose to which God has blessed me with this career."*

A key pillar of SportMantle is discipleship—building athletes who understand their identity beyond the game. Through regular meetings and outreach programs across Abuja and its environs, Victor and fellow athletes participated in initiatives centered on sharing the message of Jesus and encouraging fellow players to grow in faith.

> *"As a Discipleship family with the aim of making athletes and players grow in their relationship with Jesus, we had meetings and programs around Abuja and its environs—preaching Jesus and helping them come to the knowledge of truth."*

Victor also spoke about living out his faith within professional spaces:

> *"I’ve had the privilege of sharing my faith with some of my teammates and friends, planting seeds of the Gospel in their hearts, even as I continue to grow in my relationship and intimacy with Christ."*

## Page 11: When Athletes Pray 1.0 (2023)
*Sportmantle Magazine | Page 10*

### **WHEN ATHLETES PRAY 1.0 (2023)**

![When Athletes Pray 1.0](magazine_assets/page_11_img_01.jpeg)

In 2023, SportMantle hosted its very first corporate prayer gathering titled **“When Athletes Pray 1.0”**—a landmark moment that marked the beginning of a powerful spiritual movement within our community.

The meeting brought together athletes united by one purpose: to seek God beyond the field of play. It was a time of heartfelt worship, reflection, and intercession—where players, coaches, and mentors gathered not as competitors, but as one family grounded in faith.

“When Athletes Pray 1.0” set the tone for what SportMantle represents—an organization committed not only to developing physical strength and competitive excellence, but also nurturing spiritual depth and character. It reminded every athlete present that success is not defined solely by trophies and titles, but by alignment with purpose and dependence on God.

That first prayer meeting in 2023 became a foundation stone for the discipleship culture we continue to build today—proving that when athletes pray, they grow stronger in spirit, united in vision, and empowered for greater impact both on and off the field.

## Page 12: When Athletes Pray 2.0 (2024)
*Sportmantle Magazine | Page 11*

### **WHEN ATHLETES PRAY 2.0 (2024)**

![When Athletes Pray 2.0](magazine_assets/page_12_img_01.jpeg)

Following the powerful foundation laid in 2023, **When Athletes Pray 2.0 (2024)** marked a deeper and stronger expression of SportMantle’s commitment to spiritual growth among athletes.

Held in 2024, this second edition gathered athletes, coaches, and mentors once again in an atmosphere of intense worship, prayer, and spiritual renewal. It was more than a meeting—it was a movement of hearts aligned in surrender, gratitude, and hunger for God’s direction in their athletic journeys.

“When Athletes Pray 2.0” emphasized spiritual consistency, purpose, and identity. Athletes were reminded that their talents are gifts entrusted to them for a greater calling. There were moments of intercession for careers, families, communities, and the nation, alongside teachings that encouraged discipline, integrity, and boldness in faith.

Compared to the inaugural edition, 2.0 reflected growth—not only in attendance but in spiritual maturity. Testimonies were shared, faith was strengthened, and a renewed sense of mission was ignited among participants.

As SportMantle continues to build athletes holistically—physically, mentally, and spiritually—*When Athletes Pray* remains a cornerstone initiative, affirming that **true strength begins on our knees**.

### **AFLAME, 2024**
*Prayer Meeting, 2024*

![AFLAME Prayer Meeting](magazine_assets/page_13_img_01.jpeg)

AFLAME was more than a gathering—it was a call to spiritual awakening. Athletes came together in unity, seeking renewal, direction, and deeper intimacy with God. The atmosphere was marked by heartfelt worship, fervent prayers, and moments of reflection that stirred hearts toward greater commitment both in faith and in purpose.

The meeting emphasized living boldly for Christ in competitive spaces, maintaining integrity under pressure, and recognizing that athletic platforms can be instruments for impact and influence. Participants were reminded that true fire is sustained not just in public moments of victory, but in private moments of prayer and devotion.

AFLAME 2024 strengthened the discipleship culture of SportMantle, reinforcing our mission to raise athletes who are not only physically strong and mentally resilient, but spiritually alive and purpose-driven.

## Page 19: Visionary Profile — Joshua Paul Dandaldi
*Sportmantle Magazine | Page 18*

![Visionary Joshua Paul Dandaldi](magazine_assets/page_19_img_01.jpeg)

### **SPORTMANTLE**
> *"I want to see the Gospel spread in the sports industry."*

#### **Visionary: Joshua Paul Dandaldi**
*SportMantle Founder & Lead*

Paul Joshua is a believer in The Lord Jesus Christ, a husband, a sports consultant, and generally a sports enthusiast.

For most parts of his teenage years, he has been an active football player for various football clubs and academies across **Abuja, Nasarawa, Lagos, and Ekiti**.

As an astute lover of God, his heart burns with the desire to see the Gospel spread in the sports industry, and this conviction birthed **SportMantle**.

Paul’s vision and mission has always been:
- To preach the gospel of Christ to athletes across the world.
- To see that athletes are transformed and live a purposeful life through the renewal of their minds by the Word of God.
